Never scramble for
"what did you do this week?" again

Taskfly is a small desktop app that logs your work as you go. Type a quick note from the tray, and when standup or review time comes around, Taskfly turns your notes into a summary. It uses your own AI key, and it shows you exactly what will be sent before anything leaves your computer.

Download for macOS Download for Windows

Free and open source (GPLv3). No account. No tracking.
On macOS, builds are not notarized yet. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open.

Made for people who have to report their work

Log in two keystrokes

Click the tray icon, type, hit Enter. You can also capture your clipboard from anywhere with a keyboard shortcut.

Standups and brag docs

Turn your notes into a daily summary, a standup update, a weekly review, or bullet points for your performance review.

Share in one click

Send a finished summary straight to Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Your AI, not ours

Bring your own key. Use OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, a company gateway, or run a local model with Ollama. There is no Taskfly server.

Search and streaks

Search everything you have ever logged, and watch your streak grow on the activity heatmap.

Markdown you own

Export any range as plain markdown files. Your log is a folder of text files that belong to you.

Your notes stay on your computer

  • Nothing is recorded unless you ask. Taskfly never watches your clipboard or your screen in the background. It only captures when you press the shortcut or type a note.
  • Everything is stored locally. Your notes live in a database on your machine. There is no account, no sync, and no tracking of any kind.
  • You see what gets sent. Before a summary is generated, Taskfly strips out anything that looks like a password or an API key, then shows you the exact text so you can approve it or cancel.
  • You can check all of this yourself. The whole app is open source under GPLv3. Read the code, build it from source, or see the privacy page for the details in plain English.

Free forever. Kept alive by donations.

Taskfly has no subscription and nothing locked behind a paywall. You bring your own AI key, so there is nothing to charge you for. If it saves you time, a small donation helps keep it going.
