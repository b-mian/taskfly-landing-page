Privacy and data flow

This page explains what Taskfly does with your data, in plain English. Taskfly is open source under GPLv3, so you can check every claim on this page against the code.

What Taskfly stores, and where

  • Your notes live in a SQLite database inside your system's application data folder. They stay on your machine, except in the two cases listed below.
  • Your AI API key is stored in your operating system's keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager). It is never written to a plain file, and it is never sent to us. There is no "us" to send it to, because Taskfly has no server.
  • Settings like your model, endpoint, shortcuts, and webhook URLs live in a local settings.json file.
  • Markdown exports are only written when you click Export, into a local folder you can open any time.

When Taskfly reads your clipboard

Only when you press the capture shortcut or click Capture. There is no background clipboard polling, no screen recording, and no keylogging. If you never press capture, Taskfly never reads anything.

Every network request Taskfly can make

  1. AI summaries. Sent directly to the endpoint you set up yourself: OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, a company gateway, or a local model through Ollama (in that last case, nothing leaves your machine at all). Before any request, Taskfly strips out anything that looks like a secret (API keys, tokens, private keys, card numbers) and shows you the exact text for approval. Nothing is ever sent without that review.
  2. Slack and Teams sharing. Only when you click Share, and only to the webhook URL you configured yourself.

That is the complete list. No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no update pings (updates are manual downloads for now), and no accounts.

What your AI provider sees

Summaries are processed by the provider you picked, under your own agreement with them. For example, OpenAI does not train on API data by default. If your employer has an approved endpoint, like Azure OpenAI or an internal gateway, point Taskfly at it in Settings. If you want nothing to leave your machine, use a local model with Ollama.

Deleting your data

You can delete notes inside the app, or remove the whole application data folder to erase everything at once. Removing your API key in Settings deletes it from the system keychain.
